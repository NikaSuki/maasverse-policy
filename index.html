Privacy Policy – Maasverse

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Maasverse is a mobile application designed to help users keep track of books, characters, ratings and personal notes related to the works of Sarah J. Maas.

1. Information We Collect

Maasverse does not collect, store or process personal information from its users.

The application does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number or location.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Some application preferences and information created while using Maasverse may be stored locally on your device.

This information is not transmitted to our servers and is not accessible to us.

3. Third-Party Services

Maasverse does not use third-party services for advertising, analytics, user tracking or the collection of personal information.

4. Internet Access

Maasverse does not require an internet connection to provide its main functionality. The content included in the application is stored locally.

5. Children's Privacy

Maasverse does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality of Maasverse changes or if it becomes necessary to reflect changes in applicable requirements.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
